## Guardrails

Keep routes, canonical metadata, WordPress writes, revisions, source records and data models. Never invent publication impact, research results, dates, biographies, positions or equipment capabilities. Selection labels say “Selected publications”, not an unsupported impact-factor claim. WordPress Featured selection has priority. No new fonts, animation libraries or third-party services.

## Implemented presentation changes

- **Home:** publication evidence precedes the atomic-scale philosophy section. One larger lead paper and compact supporting papers show source journal metadata. Member previews omit full biography disclosures; news preview text is calculated on the server. Existing CMS hero copy, CTAs, metadata strip, transparent rotating gold cluster, interaction, mobile rendering limits and reduced-motion behavior are retained.
- **Research:** alternating image arrangements, consistent scientific overview hierarchy and comfortable reading widths. Existing source-backed research explanations and expandable complete overview remain available.
- **People:** equivalent Graduate Students / Graduate Researchers, Postdoctoral Fellows / Postdoctoral Researchers and Undergraduate Students / Undergraduate Researchers are grouped for display. Source roles and old category fragment IDs are preserved. Current profiles use lighter separators; Alumni has a quieter surface. Image-only bios remain intact.
- **PI:** sticky desktop portrait/professional-information column with verified office, email and professional links; static column on mobile. Biography stays within reading width. Duplicate contact paragraphs are removed only when their information is already available in the panel. Explicitly cleared profile-link fields override legacy links.
- **Publications:** year grouping remains. Search covers source title, citation (including authors/journal) and DOI. Full citations, superscripts and author marks remain available through keyboard-accessible disclosures. Nonfeatured papers use compact editorial rows; featured papers retain full citation prominence. Year links stay available while scrolling on desktop.
- **News:** source title/summary search, 12-entry progressive disclosure, result counts and meaningful no-match feedback. Existing `story-*` and `news-update-*` links automatically reveal their target. Source dates and undated archive provenance are unchanged.
- **Facilities:** approved instruments use image/detail columns that stack on phones; removed photos remain hidden. Source-only instrument photography remains available without invented names/capabilities.
- **Group Photos:** native modal dialog with Previous/Next, arrow-key support, Escape, focus containment/return, source captions and original-image links. Existing anchors retain an original-image fallback before hydration. Stable thumbnail dimensions and lazy loading are preserved.
- **Join Us:** clearer opportunity-path spacing and headings, restrained red contact-panel accent and consistent contact CTA hierarchy. Existing source-backed pathways and official links remain; no funded vacancies are implied.
- **Contact:** CMS department/university fields drive the introduction. Heading/email/directions information is not repeated in the detail body; approved building, room, mailing address, phone and other details remain.
- **Teaching:** quieter source-term hierarchy, reading width and spacing; historical course terms and the availability note are preserved.
- **Shared/footer/loading:** responsive 16–17px body text, 8px radius, 160ms transition token, input/textarea focus rings, consistent section separators and more compact footer spacing. Stable, nonanimated loading placeholders preserve a useful structure. Existing WordPress failure reporting and visitor-facing fallbacks are retained.

## Publication selection policy

**Subsequent user curation supersedes the automatic policy below:** the homepage now selects exactly the Science 2019 and JACS 2018, 2013 and 2012 citations from the supplied list. Source citations, images, DOI links and “Prior to SMU” attribution are preserved. Other papers remain in the full bibliography; publication highlighting applies there. Teaching is now directly linked in the main navigation. Page Headers provides dedicated WordPress-backed photo/upload editors for Research, Publications, News and Facilities; the approved WordPress nanocluster image is the initial Research background.

WordPress Featured choices take priority, with at most four displayed. Otherwise, use only current-group Published/Accepted records. The automatic selection reserves a representative slot for a verified JACS/Nature/Angewandte citation within five years of the newest eligible record when available, then fills up to three positions using recency, verified recognized-journal evidence and image availability. No journal is inferred from a paper under review. Volume numbers are removed from the journal badge only; the original citation is untouched.

The checked source currently supports a 2022 JACS paper, a 2025 Chemistry of Materials citation and a 2025 ChemSusChem accepted paper. These are source-derived selections, not impact-factor claims. Scientific significance is an editorial decision: the lab should use Featured flags to choose the most representative work. No publication, author, journal affiliation, DOI or scientific contribution was fabricated.

## Validation and performance evidence

The public browser suite covers all 11 routes at 375, 430, 768, 1024 and 1440px, plus automated WCAG AA checks, keyboard navigation, citation disclosures, failed images, reduced motion, WebGL fallback, lightbox focus restoration, search and deep links. Before/after captures and measurements are in `.preview/ui-before/` and `.preview/ui-after/`. The publication page's sampled phone height decreased from 18,890px to approximately 14,567px while all citations remain accessible; the initial news archive decreased from 5,863px to approximately 4,313px. These are content-dependent visual measurements, not fixed layout promises.

Production Chrome samples for Home, Publications, Gallery and People recorded CLS 0 and LCP approximately 0.43–1.68s locally. These are local samples under the test environment, not field Core Web Vitals or a Lighthouse certification. The shared client-rendered cards no longer import Cheerio merely to produce a news preview. No font download, UI library, animation dependency or third-party service was added. The 3D scene retains lazy loading and its existing instanced geometry/mobile limits.

WordPress source records, editing, media selection/upload, roles, revision restoration, cache invalidation, route paths, canonical SEO and Vercel configuration are preserved. Admin checks use the loopback WordPress test backend; this work did not write to live WordPress. The known Windows Playwright server-teardown hang requires stopping the admin runner after its assertions complete.

Automated accessibility checks supplement visual and keyboard review; they are not a claim of full WCAG certification. Check counts and final build results are recorded in the completion message.

## Remaining editorial recommendations

Use WordPress Featured flags to approve the scientifically most important papers. Complete source alt text, verified instrument models/capabilities and image-only biographies. Replace externally hosted portraits that return 403 with approved WordPress media. Research/gallery category filters should follow reliable approved metadata rather than guessed classifications. Measure production field performance after deployment and arrange a human screen-reader/content review.
